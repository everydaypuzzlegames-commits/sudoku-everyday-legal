Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how "Sudoku Everyday – Train Your Mind" ("we", "our", or "us"), published by Everyday Puzzle Games, collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

However, the app may collect limited non-personal data automatically, including:

  • Device information (device model, OS version)
  • App usage data (game progress, interactions, session duration)
  • Crash logs and diagnostics
  • Advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID)

2. Permissions We Use

Internet Access:
Required to display advertisements (e.g., Google AdMob) and support app functionality.

Notifications:
Used to send optional daily reminders if enabled by the user. Notifications can be disabled at any time via device settings.

3. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services such as:

  • Google AdMob (for ads)
  • Firebase Analytics (optional)
  • Firebase Crashlytics (optional)

These services may collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

4. How We Use Information

The collected data is used to:

  • Improve gameplay experience
  • Fix bugs and crashes
  • Analyze app performance
  • Serve relevant advertisements

5. Data Storage

  • Game progress is stored locally on your device
  • We do not store personal data on external servers
  • We do not sell or share personal information

6. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

7. User Control

You can control:

  • Notifications via device settings
  • App permissions via device settings
  • Data reset by reinstalling the app

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of transmission is 100% secure.
